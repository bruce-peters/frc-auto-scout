Label shooters

How to label

Each item is one scoring burst: the alliance's fuel counter on the scoreboard went up by the number shown. The clip plays the few seconds before the jump, because balls take about 1 to 5 s from launch to being counted.

Mark every robot that launched balls into its hub in that clip with 123, then press Enter. If several robots shot, the balls are split evenly. Use Can't tell rather than guessing, and Human player / other if no robot launched them. Bumper numbers are easiest to read in the end cameras at the bottom of the frame. The system's own boxes and credits are hidden while you label.
